# Submission guidelines

The NSLP-SDP 2027 workshop invites **methodological, resource, evaluation, system, and position papers**. We especially encourage submissions from first-time authors, students, researchers from underrepresented groups and regions, and work on non-English scholarly communication. 

* We will **not accept work that is under review or has already been published in or accepted for** publication in a journal, another conference, or another workshop.
* The workshop invites anonymous submissions of regular **long papers (up to 8 pages without references and appendix)**; **short papers** as well as **position papers (up to 4 pages without references and appendix)** presenting, for example, negative results, in-progress projects, or demos.
* Authors are permitted to include an optional appendix of up to 2 pages. However, reviewers will not be mandated to review the appendix and all papers must be self-contained.
* Submissions are expected to **discuss relevant limitations and risks** of the work, for example fabricated claims and citations, over-reliance on automated review, assessment, or writing, bias toward English and well-resourced disciplines, privacy and confidentiality, copyright, licensing and provenance, or compute and environmental costs.
* Reviewing will be performed **double-blind**. Each submission will receive **three reviews**.
* Submissions **must be in PDF**, formatted in the **WebConf 2027 style**.
* The proceedings of this workshop will be published as part of the **WebConf 2027 companion proceedings**.
* NSLP-SDP 2027 is an **in-person workshop**. At least **one author per contribution must register** for the workshop and present in person.
* All submissions are done via **OpenReview** – link will be provided soon.
